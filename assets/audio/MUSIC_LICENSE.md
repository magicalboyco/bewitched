# Bewitched audio asset rights

**Moonlit Apothecary** is an original composition created specifically for Bewitched, represented as MIDI note events in `src/music/zen-score.js`. No copyrighted third-party melodies, recordings, soundfonts, or samples were used.

The renderer should synthesize the notes with Web Audio rather than using commercial samples. Future audio files may be added after separate license review. The score can be modified to fit gameplay and remain inside this project.

Music playback should begin only after a user gesture, with independent music/SFX mute and volume; fade down during pause and stop cleanly when leaving gameplay.
